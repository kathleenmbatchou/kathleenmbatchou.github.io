AI Study Coach Agent

Systems Analysis & Design · Aug–Dec 2026

The goal

Build an AI study coach that helps students prepare for their own courses.

My role

I manage the team’s scope, milestones, and deliverables, and turn stakeholder pain points into requirements with clear success criteria.

Responsible AI

We set privacy, data-handling, and quality-control rules up front so the agent’s answers stay accurate and trustworthy.

24stakeholders piloted it: my classmates and professor
63%found it helpful (15 of 24)
33%asked for more features (8 of 24)
Proposed v2 design · not built yet

What users asked for next: Canvas integration

  • Syncs to each course syllabus and updates when the course changes
  • Reminds students about upcoming exams
  • In study mode, quizzes students on the material they’ll be tested on

What I learned

Talking to students before we built the agent showed me how hard studying is when you’re balancing school with responsibilities outside of it. It’s easy to let AI do the work for you. I learned that helping students see AI as a tool for learning is what actually empowers them, and that I can build something that changes how people learn and understand material.
